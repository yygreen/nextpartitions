## How their installation videos are made (from "Toilet Partition Installation of Phenolic", 5:05, watched frame by frame)

- Plain grey 3D model, no narration, no captions; on-screen text is only the kit titles ("Use Pilaster Bracket Kit", "Use two ear Panel to Pilaster Kit", "Use Pilaster Mounting Hardware", "Use Outswing Door Kit", "Use Inswing Door Kit", "Use Headrail Kit") over a white card showing the kit's parts.
- Order: layout with centre lines on the wall and floor → wall brackets (holes, plugs, screws) → floor studs, nuts and washers → panels on blocks (a height dimension shown) → pilaster brackets → pilasters onto the studs with the leveling bracket and shoe → panel-to-pilaster brackets and through-bolts → outswing door hardware piece by piece (hinges, latch, keeper, pull, coat hook, bumper) → inswing door hardware → headrail brackets, headrail, end cap, wall bracket.
- Every fastener goes in on screen, but there's no explanation of why: no backing, no leveling or plumb checks, no door-gap adjustment, no final check.

## What this means for ours

- **Detail:** match their hardware detail (kit cards, every fastener at least once in close-up), and keep what they lack: captions in plain words, labels, backing, leveling and plumb guides, door adjustment, final checks.
- **Coverage they don't have:** floor mounted, ceiling hung and floor-to-ceiling. Our four mounting-style videos fill that gap.
- **Coverage they do have and we don't yet:** materials (the hardware differs: steel uses formed brackets and panels with honeycomb cores; solid plastic uses continuous brackets and wrap or continuous hinges; phenolic and laminate use angle brackets) and layouts (alcove, in-corner). Our scene code takes the layout and material as data, so these become variations of the same build, not new videos from scratch.
- **Suggested order after the four mounting styles:** phenolic and powder coated steel alcove (their most watched), then solid plastic (continuous brackets and hinges), then in-corner.
