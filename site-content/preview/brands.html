Toilet Partition Manufacturers We Quote

Seven toilet partition manufacturers make most of the commercial stalls in US restrooms, and we quote all seven: Bobrick, ASI Global, Scranton Products, Bradley, Metpar, Ammco and Hadrian.

This page compares them by material and series so you can see who makes what, then points you to each brand's page. Next Partitions sells partitions; it does not install them.

Series names are the manufacturer's, not ours. Bobrick's DuraLine, for example, is compact grade laminate, which is phenolic, and not a solid plastic line. Bobrick describes its materials as compact grade laminate, solid color reinforced composite and high pressure laminate, and compares them with powder coated steel, HDPE and stainless steel rather than offering those three.

How the brands differ

Bobrick spans several laminate-based materials, and its data sheets give mounting series, heights and anchors in detail. ASI Global publishes installation guides for its steel, stainless and solid plastic partitions, which helps when you are replacing parts or planning a job. Scranton Products is the solid plastic specialist among the seven. Its Hiny Hiders line is offered floor mounted overhead-braced, ceiling hung or floor to ceiling, and its Eclipse line has shiplap door edges.

Bradley offers phenolic and stainless steel in four mounting styles (Series 400 to 700). Its phenolic comes with 58 inch doors and panels at 12 inches above the floor, or 69 or 72 inch at 9 or 6 inches, with an integral no-sightline design as standard, and its stainless steel has a Permaseal edge. Bradley also makes washroom accessories and handwashing systems. Metpar and Ammco both make powder coated steel, which Metpar calls its most economical and most popular material, and both also list stainless steel and phenolic. Metpar adds Dur-A-Tex, a textured steel with a two-coat paint process, and Ammco also lists solid plastic and plastic laminate. Hadrian, a Zurn Elkay brand, makes partitions in four materials and several mounting styles; see the Hadrian page for how we handle Hadrian projects.

Choosing between brands

Pick the material and mounting style first, then compare brands. The brand matters most in three cases:

  • Replacing parts. Hinge and latch hole patterns, pilaster thickness and fasteners differ between makers. You need the brand to match a part. See hardware and parts.
  • Matching existing partitions. An addition to a restroom should come from the same maker if the colors and hardware must match.
  • Project specifications. Many specs name a series. Send us the spec and we will quote what it calls for, or a comparable product if you allow substitutions.

Not sure which brand you have? Contact us with a photo of the hinge and latch.

Compare by material

  • Powder coated steel: Metpar, Ammco, ASI Global, Hadrian, and Bradley (shown in its installation guides, not in its 2025 brochure).
  • Solid plastic (HDPE): Scranton Products, ASI Global, Ammco, Hadrian.
  • Phenolic: Bobrick, Bradley, Metpar, Ammco, ASI Global, Hadrian.
  • Plastic laminate: Bobrick, ASI Global, Ammco.
  • Stainless steel: ASI Global, Bradley, Metpar, Ammco, Hadrian.

If solid plastic is the choice and the restroom is in a building covered by a code based on the International Building Code, ask for the fire-rated version: HDPE used as an interior finish must pass NFPA 286, and not every color does.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who are the main toilet partition manufacturers?

Bobrick, ASI Global, Scranton Products, Bradley, Metpar, Ammco (All American Metal) and Hadrian are the seven we quote.

Which brand makes solid plastic partitions?

Scranton Products makes solid plastic (HDPE) as its core line, including Hiny Hiders, Eclipse and Aria. ASI Global, Ammco and Hadrian also list solid plastic partitions.

Is Bobrick DuraLine solid plastic?

No. Bobrick describes DuraLine as compact grade laminate, which is a phenolic material.

Can I mix brands in one restroom?

You can, but hinges, latches and pilasters are not interchangeable across brands. Keep one brand per run of stalls.

Do you install partitions?

No. Follow the manufacturer's instructions and approved drawings, and use a qualified installer.
