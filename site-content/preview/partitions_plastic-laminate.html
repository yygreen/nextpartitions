Plastic Laminate Bathroom Partitions

Plastic laminate bathroom partitions bond a decorative high-pressure laminate (HPL) to a wood-based core.

Their appeal is color and pattern choice. Their limit is water. This page covers how they are built, the published fire class (Class B at Bobrick, not Class A), how warranties differ by brand, and where laminate fits.

How plastic laminate partitions are built

Bobrick specifies a 45 lb density, resin-impregnated particleboard core, and its HPL doors, stiles and panels are 1" thick (1040 Series, DesignerSeries HPL). ASI sizes its headrail for laminate at 1-1/4", so ASI's pilasters are thicker than Bobrick's.

ASI's version adds Moisture Guard edge banding that fuses with the core.

  1. Core: resin-impregnated particleboard, 45 lb density.
  2. Faces: high-pressure decorative laminate bonded to both faces.
  3. Edge: an edge band sealing the core; some lines fuse it to the core for moisture resistance.

Fire rating

Bobrick lists its high-pressure laminate as Class B (flame spread 60) in its 2018 CEU presentation. Class B is a flame spread index of 26 to 75 under ASTM E84. The current page's "Class A standard" is therefore not right for Bobrick's laminate. Other brands may differ. Ask for the rating of the line and decor you pick.

Warranty differs by brand

We hold no manufacturer warranty document for plastic laminate. The current page said 2 years in one place and 5 years in another, and neither can be traced to a brand. Your quote will name the brand, product line and warranty length. Read the warranty text, since coverage for moisture damage in particular varies.

Moisture, vandal resistance and cleaning

  • Moisture. Bobrick notes that the core "may swell when exposed to excessive moisture". Keep laminate in dry, climate-controlled restrooms, not showers, pools or locker rooms. Use phenolic or HDPE there.
  • Scratches. Bobrick lists scratch, dent and graffiti resistance at a low cost. A deep scratch can expose the dark kraft paper under the surface.
  • Cleaning. Follow the manufacturer's care instructions. We don't hold a manufacturer cleaning specification.

Where laminate fits

Offices, retail, restaurants and other dry restrooms where the partitions should coordinate with millwork. Color range is the main advantage. Bobrick lists a wide color selection. Bobrick's 1550 Series offers floor-anchored (1551), overhead-braced and ceiling-hung (1558) mounting. See the mounting styles.

Bobrick's ceiling-hung guidance requires ceiling structure that supports at least 295 lb per high pressure laminate partition (TB-32).

What comes with a quote

Our quotes cover panels, doors, pilasters, headrails, hinges, slide latches, keepers, strikes, brackets, pilaster shoes and mounting fasteners. Install according to the manufacturer's instructions and your approved drawings, using a qualified installer.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is plastic laminate partition made of?

High-pressure laminate bonded to a resin-impregnated particleboard core (Bobrick).

How thick are plastic laminate partitions?

Bobrick's doors, stiles and panels are 1" thick. ASI uses 1-1/4" pilasters, judging from its headrail sizing.

Are plastic laminate partitions Class A?

Bobrick lists its HPL as Class B (flame spread 60). Check the brand's rating.

Can plastic laminate get wet?

Not continuously. The core may swell with excessive moisture (Bobrick). Use phenolic or HDPE in wet rooms.

What is the warranty?

It depends on brand and line. We confirm it at quote time.
