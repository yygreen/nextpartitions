 The widest color and pattern selection in commercial bathroom partitions — over 200 finishes including woodgrains, stones, and solid colors.
[Configure Plastic Laminate](/get-a-quote?material=plastic-laminate)
## Product Overview
 Plastic laminate partitions wrap a high-pressure decorative laminate around a particleboard core, finished with a moisture-resistant edge band. They deliver the broadest design palette of any partition material — over 200 woodgrains, stones, and solid colors from Wilsonart, Formica, and Nevamar — making them the right choice when the restroom is part of a designed interior rather than a back-of-house utility space.
- 200+ standard colors, woodgrains, and stone patterns
- Design coordination with countertops and casework
- Class A fire rating standard
- Smooth, easy-to-clean surface
- Lighter weight than steel or HDPE — simpler installation
- Best for low-moisture interior restrooms
## Best Applications
 Corporate offices, hotel back-of-house, boutique retail, restaurants, and any restroom where the partition is expected to coordinate with millwork or interior finishes. Avoid plastic laminate in pools, locker rooms, or any environment with sustained humidity — spec HDPE or phenolic instead.
