## About Scranton Products
 Scranton Products manufactures HDPE solid plastic toilet partitions in Scranton, Pennsylvania, and is the dominant US brand in school and recreation restrooms. The Hiny Hiders line set the modern standard for K-12 partitions; Aria, Eclipse, and Resistall extend the platform with full-height privacy doors, premium aesthetics, and reinforced cores for high-vandalism environments.
