## About ASI Global
 American Specialties (ASI) acquired Global Partitions in 2010 and now operates one of the most complete commercial bathroom partition portfolios in North America. The Global product family covers every material and mounting style, which makes ASI Global the most common spec on multi-building projects where consistency matters more than chasing the absolute lowest unit cost.
## ASI Global Product Lines
- Powder Coated Steel
 Standard 1" panel, 1-1/4" pilaster construction in 16+ colors.
- Solid Plastic (HDPE)

- Phenolic
 Black core and color-thru phenolic for high-traffic public restrooms.
- Plastic Laminate
 Wilsonart and Formica laminate selections for design-driven offices.
- Stainless Steel
 #4 satin and embossed stainless for upscale commercial work.
