 Bobrick Washroom Equipment has set the global standard for commercial washroom products for over a century. ## About Bobrick
 Founded in 1906, Bobrick is the most widely specified washroom brand in North America. Their partition portfolio is intentionally broad so architects can hold a single spec across powder coated steel, stainless, HDPE, phenolic, and plastic laminate without changing manufacturers. Bobrick partitions pair seamlessly with the company's accessory and fixture lines, which simplifies sourcing on multi-trade commercial projects.
## Bobrick Product Lines
- DuraLine Solid Plastic (HDPE)
 Color-thru HDPE in 17+ standard colors. Vandal-resistant, moisture-proof, ideal for schools and recreation.
- SierraSeries Powder Coated Steel
 Honeycomb-core powder coated steel with 16 standard colors. The standard spec for offices and retail.
- 1080 Series Stainless Steel
 Type 304 stainless in #4 satin or leathergrain emboss for premium commercial restrooms.
- 1090 Phenolic
 Black-core and color-thru phenolic for the most demanding wet environments.
- Plastic Laminate
 Wilsonart and Formica laminate selections for design-driven offices and hospitality.
