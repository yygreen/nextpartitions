# Frequently Asked Questions
 Answers to the most common questions about commercial bathroom partitions, ordering, and shipping.
## How much do bathroom partitions cost?
 Commercial bathroom partition pricing depends on material, mounting style, color, dimensions, and stall count. Powder coated steel typically starts around $275/stall while stainless steel and wood reach $900–$1,200/stall. Use our configurator for an exact quote.
## Why do bathroom stalls have gaps?
 Floor gaps (typically 12 inches) make cleaning easier, allow plumbing access, support occupancy checks, and let users signal an emergency. Door gaps reduce manufacturing cost and improve airflow.
## What is the standard bathroom stall size?
 Standard stalls are 36 inches wide by 60 inches deep with a 58-inch panel height and a 12-inch floor gap. ADA-compliant stalls are 60 inches wide by at least 56 inches deep with a 32-inch minimum clear door opening.
## What materials are bathroom partitions made of?
 The six common materials are powder coated steel, solid plastic (HDPE), phenolic, plastic laminate, stainless steel, and wood. Each balances cost, durability, moisture resistance, and design flexibility differently.
## Do you ship bathroom partitions nationwide?
 Yes. We ship commercial bathroom partitions nationwide via freight carrier, with standard and expedited options plus a liftgate add-on for locations without a loading dock.
## Can I order ADA-compliant bathroom stalls?
 Yes. Our configurator includes an ADA toggle that automatically adjusts dimensions, door width, and grab bar requirements per ADA bathroom standards.
## How long does it take to receive bathroom partitions?
 Stock items ship in 48–72 hours. Custom configurations typically deliver in 10–15 business days depending on material, color, and freight destination.
## Do you accept purchase orders?
 Yes. We accept purchase orders for tax-exempt organizations including schools, government buyers, and houses of worship, plus credit card and ACH/bank transfer.
