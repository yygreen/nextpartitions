# Door Hardware
 Handles, pulls, strikes, keepers, door stops, and door bumpers for commercial bathroom stalls.
[Contact Us](/contact)
## Door Hardware Components
 Every stall door uses three or four pieces of hardware that wear out independently — the handle or pull, the strike that the latch engages, the keeper that limits door travel, and the bumper that protects the door panel from latch contact. We stock all four for every major manufacturer, so you can rebuild a tired door without replacing the panel.
- Door pulls and handles (chrome, satin, stainless)
- Latch strikes and keepers
- Door stops and bumpers
- Coat hook + bumper combos
- ADA paddle latches and full-length pulls
- Continuous piano hinges
## Specifying for Your Brand
 ASI/Global, Bobrick, Scranton Products, Metpar, and Ammco each use slightly different hole patterns and fastener spacings. Send us a photo of the existing hardware and the partition manufacturer label (typically inside the door) and we'll cross-reference the right part.
