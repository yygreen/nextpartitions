# Partitions for Offices
 Professional restroom partitions for corporate environments. Coordinate finishes with the rest of your interior package.
[Get a Quote](/get-a-quote)
 Office restrooms see steady but gentle traffic, so the spec emphasizes appearance, privacy, and finish coordination over extreme durability. The restroom is one of the few spaces every employee and visitor uses, which makes it a disproportionate driver of how the workplace feels.
 We supply corporate campuses, coworking operators, and property managers with partition packages that match the interior design package, ship as complete floor-by-floor kits, and install with minimal disruption to tenants.
## What Offices Need
- Finishes that coordinate with millwork, casework, and tile 
- Higher-privacy options for modern workplace expectations 
- Quiet operation — soft-close or emergency-access hardware 
- Clean, minimal profiles and concealed fasteners 
- Fast, low-disruption installation in occupied buildings 
- ADA-compliant compartments on every floor 
- Consistent spec that can be repeated across floors and sites 
- Easy maintenance with standard janitorial products

## Planning & Compliance
- Standard stalls are 36 inches wide; ADA compartments are 60 inches wide with a 36-inch outswing door. 
- Standard panels are 55 inches high at 12 inches off the floor; privacy specs go to 72 inches at 6 inches off the floor. 
- Coordinate partition color with tile, wall covering, and vanity tops before ordering. 
- Confirm slab and stud conditions in older buildings before choosing a mounting style. 
- Repeat one spec across floors so maintenance can keep a single hardware kit on hand.
