# Solid Plastic (HDPE) Partitions
 High-density polyethylene partitions purpose-built for moisture, impact, and graffiti resistance. The dominant choice for schools, gyms, and aquatic facilities.
[Configure HDPE](/get-a-quote?material=solid-plastic)
## Product Overview
 Solid plastic HDPE partitions are produced from a single, homogeneous polymer panel — no core, no skin, no laminate. The result is a partition that cannot rust, will not delaminate, resists impact and graffiti, and can be power-washed daily without breaking down. HDPE has become the default partition material for K–12 schools, universities, gyms, locker rooms, pool facilities, and any restroom that gets hosed out as part of routine cleaning.
- 100% solid HDPE — no skin or core to fail
- Will not rust, even in chlorinated pool environments
- Graffiti wipes off; deep marks sand out
- Resists impact, moisture, mildew, and most chemicals
- Recyclable; many lines contain post-consumer content
- 25-year manufacturer warranty
## Best Applications
 Schools, universities, recreation centers, gyms, locker rooms, swimming pools, beach facilities, parks departments, and any high-moisture or vandalism-sensitive restroom. HDPE is also the right choice when budget priorities favor lifetime cost over upfront price — the 25-year warranty typically pays for itself versus replacing powder coated steel two or three times.
