# Ammco / All American Metal Partitions
 Ammco (All American Metal Corp) is a value-focused manufacturer of commercial bathroom partitions. Their powder coated steel and stainless lines deliver competitive pricing for budget-sensitive projects without giving up the structural specs facility managers expect.

## About Ammco
 All American Metal Corp (Ammco) builds commercial bathroom partitions designed to hit aggressive price points on retrofit, multi-family, and tenant-improvement work. The product is straightforward — honeycomb-core powder coated steel and stainless — with the same standard mounting options as the larger brands. If your project is budget-driven and you don't need a premium nameplate, Ammco frequently wins on total installed cost.
## Ammco Product Lines
- Powder Coated Steel
 Honeycomb-core powder coated steel partitions in standard commercial colors at value pricing.
- Stainless Steel
 Type 304 stainless for high-moisture and upscale environments without premium markup.
## Why Pick Ammco
- Highly competitive pricing — often the lowest-cost option in steel
- Standard 1" panel / 1-1/4" pilaster construction
- Available in overhead braced and floor mounted
- Good fit for retrofit, multi-family, and budget-driven TI work
