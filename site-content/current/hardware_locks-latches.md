# Locks & Latches
 Bathroom stall locks, slide bolt latches, indicator locks, and ADA-compliant privacy hardware. Available in chrome, satin, and stainless finishes for every partition brand.
[Contact Us](/contact)
## Lock & Latch Types
 Slide Bolt Latch
 The standard chrome- or stainless-finished slide bolt found in most US commercial restrooms. Quietly engages a wall-mounted keeper.
 Indicator Latch (Vacant / Occupied)
 Slide bolt with a colored indicator wheel that flips from green (vacant) to red (occupied). Required spec in many airports and stadiums.
 ADA Privacy Latch
 Larger paddle-style latch operable with a closed fist or elbow. Required for ADA-compliant stalls per ICC A117.1.
 Surface-Mounted Coat Hook + Bumper
 Combination unit installed on the door interior. Doubles as a bump stop to protect the door surface from contact with the latch keeper.
## Replacement & Compatibility
 We stock replacement locks and latches for ASI/Global, Bobrick, Scranton Products, Metpar, and Ammco. Tell us your manufacturer (or send a photo) and we'll cross-reference the correct part number. Most slide bolts and keepers are interchangeable across brands when paired with the right strike, so don't replace an entire stall just because the latch failed.
