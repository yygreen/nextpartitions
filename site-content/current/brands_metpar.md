# Metpar Toilet Partitions
 Metpar has manufactured commercial bathroom partitions in Long Island, New York since 1957. Their catalog covers powder coated steel, stainless, solid plastic HDPE, and phenolic — frequently specified on East Coast K-12, healthcare, and government projects.

## About Metpar
 Metpar Corp builds partitions in Westbury, NY and has been a fixture on East Coast specifications for over six decades. Their product range covers every common partition material, and their pricing is often more competitive than the larger national brands without sacrificing build quality. We're an authorized Metpar distributor and ship the full catalog nationwide.
## Metpar Product Lines
- Powder Coated Steel
 Honeycomb-core powder coated steel partitions in 18 standard colors with a 3-year warranty.
- Stainless Steel
 Type 304 #4 satin and embossed stainless for upscale and high-moisture environments.
- Solid Plastic (HDPE)
 Color-thru HDPE in 14+ colors, ideal for K-12 schools and aquatic centers.
- Phenolic (Color-Thru & Black Core)
 Premium phenolic partitions for high-traffic public restrooms and gyms.
