 Complete reference for ADA-compliant commercial restrooms: stall sizing, grab bar placement, door clearances, toilet height, and approach clearances.

## Why ADA Compliance Matters
 The Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and the ICC A117.1 accessibility standard set minimum requirements for commercial restrooms. Failing to meet them exposes building owners to lawsuits, occupancy permit denials, and costly retrofits. Every commercial restroom in the US must include at least one ADA-compliant stall, and the partitions are a key part of the spec.
## ADA Stall Dimensions
 | | Requirement | Dimension 
 | Minimum stall width | 60" 
 | Minimum depth (wall-hung WC) | 56" 
 | Minimum depth (floor-mounted WC) | 59" 
 | Minimum clear door opening | 32" 
 | Door swing | Outswing or sliding (may swing in only if the compartment is at least 60" deep beyond the door swing) 
 | Toilet centerline from side wall | 16–18" 
 | Ambulatory accessible compartment | 35–37" wide × 60" deep minimum 
## Grab Bars
- Side-wall grab bar: 42" minimum length, mounted 33–36" above finished floor
- Side-wall bar must start 12" maximum from the rear wall and extend at least 54" from the rear wall
- Rear-wall grab bar: 36" minimum length, extending 12" min on one side and 24" min on the other side of the toilet centerline
- 1-1/4" to 2" diameter, with 1-1/2" clearance from the wall
- Must support a 250 lb static load in any direction
- Partition panels at the grab-bar wall must be reinforced for the load
## Door Hardware
 ADA stall door hardware must be operable with one hand, without tight grasping, pinching, or twisting. That rules out the standard slide bolt and requires a paddle latch or U-pull. See our [locks & latches](/hardware/locks-latches) page for ADA-compliant options.
## Frequently Asked Questions
 What is the minimum size of an ADA bathroom stall?
 An ADA-compliant stall is a minimum 60" wide by 56" deep for wall-hung toilets, or 60" wide by 59" deep for floor-mounted toilets, with a 32" minimum clear door opening.
 How high should grab bars be installed?
 Grab bars must be mounted 33–36" above the finished floor, with a 42" minimum side-wall bar and a 36" minimum rear-wall bar.
 Do ADA stall doors have to swing out?
 Yes. ADA-compliant stall doors must swing outward, or be a sliding door, so the user inside has full unobstructed access to the toilet and grab bars.
 What is the required toilet height for ADA?
 ADA water closets must have a seat 17–19" above the finished floor, measured to the top of the toilet seat.
