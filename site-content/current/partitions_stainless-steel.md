# Stainless Steel Partitions
 The upscale partition material for airports, hotels, hospitals, and corporate restrooms where appearance and durability are equally important.
[Configure Stainless Steel](/get-a-quote?material=stainless-steel)
## Product Overview
 Stainless steel partitions wrap a honeycomb core in 22-gauge type 304 stainless steel, finished in either a #4 brushed satin or a leathergrain emboss that hides handprints and minor scratches. The combination of premium aesthetic, full corrosion resistance, and easy sanitation makes stainless the standard for transit, hospitality, and healthcare restrooms where the space reads as a public-facing finish, not a back-of-house utility.
- Type 304 stainless steel skin, 22 ga
- #4 satin brushed or leathergrain emboss
- Will not rust, even in coastal or pool environments
- Hygienic surface — standard in healthcare
- Available in all four mounting styles
## Best Applications
 Airports, train stations, hotels, hospitals, surgical centers, upscale restaurants, corporate headquarters, and any restroom where the partition is part of the brand experience. Embossed leathergrain is preferred in high-touch facilities because it disguises smudges between cleanings.
