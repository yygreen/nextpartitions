# Replacement Stall Doors
 Custom-sized replacement bathroom stall doors in every material. Match an existing partition without replacing the entire system.
[Contact Us](/contact)
## Replace Just the Door
 When a stall door is dented, vandalized, or warped, you don't have to replace the entire partition system. We custom-fabricate replacement doors in every material we sell, color-matched to the closest standard or pre-existing custom finish. Send us the door dimensions, hinge cut-out locations, and the manufacturer of the existing partitions, and we'll quote a one-for-one replacement that drops into your existing pilasters.
- Powder coated steel — 16+ standard colors
- Solid plastic HDPE — 20+ solid colors and patterns
- Phenolic — standard and premium color lines
- Plastic laminate — 200+ patterns and woodgrains
- Stainless steel — #4 brushed or leathergrain emboss
- ADA outswing configurations available
## What We Need to Quote
- 1. Door width and height (measure existing door, not the rough opening)
- 2. Material and color of the existing partitions
- 3. Manufacturer brand (check inside the door for a label)
- 4. Hinge type — gravity, continuous
- 5. Photos of the existing door, latch, and pilaster mounting
