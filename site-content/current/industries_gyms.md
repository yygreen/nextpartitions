# Partitions for Gyms & Fitness Facilities
 Moisture-, chlorine-, and humidity-resistant partitions for locker rooms, pools, spa areas, and shower rooms.
[Get a Quote](/get-a-quote)
 Pool decks, locker rooms, and shower areas combine humidity, chlorine vapor, and aggressive cleaning chemistry. Powder coated steel rusts at the edges within a few seasons and plastic laminate delaminates as moisture wicks into the particleboard core. HDPE solid plastic ignores all of it.
 We supply gyms, fitness franchises, aquatic centers, and university athletics with waterproof partition, urinal screen, and shower cubicle packages that can be hosed down every day of their service life.
## What Gyms & Aquatic Facilities Need
- 100% waterproof panels with no absorbent core 
- Chlorine and humidity resistance around pools and spas 
- Daily power-wash and disinfectant tolerance 
- Stainless or polymer hardware that will not pit 
- Mounting that keeps the floor clear for squeegeeing 
- Colors and patterns that hide soap scum and chalk 
- Matching urinal screens, shower stalls, and changing cubicles 
- Long warranties for high-humidity service

## Planning & Compliance
- Standard stalls are 36 inches wide; ADA compartments are 60 inches wide with a 36-inch outswing door. 
- Aquatic facilities usually raise panel heights and lower floor clearance for added privacy in changing areas. 
- Specify stainless brackets, hinges, and latches — not zinc — anywhere chlorine is present. 
- Include urinal screens and matching shower cubicles in the same package for a consistent finish. 
- Verify slab drainage and slope before choosing floor mounted over ceiling hung.
