# Bradley Corporation Partitions
 Bradley Corporation is a Menomonee Falls, Wisconsin manufacturer of commercial washroom products with more than a century in the industry. Their partition portfolio spans Mills HDPE solid plastic, Lenox powder coated steel, and stainless — paired with Bradley washroom accessories, Verge and WashBar handwashing systems, and Advocate multi-user lavatories.

## About Bradley
 Founded in 1921, Bradley Corporation is one of the most established names in commercial washroom design. Their partition brands — Mills and Lenox — cover the two most-specified materials in the US market, and the broader Bradley catalog lets a project source partitions, accessories, and washfountains from a single manufacturer.
## Bradley Partition Lines
- Mills Solid Plastic (HDPE)
 Vandal-resistant HDPE with a 25-year warranty. Standard in K-12, parks, and high-humidity applications.
- Lenox Powder Coated Steel
 Value-engineered powder coated steel — the workhorse partition for offices, retail, and light commercial.
- Lenox Stainless Steel
 Type 304 stainless for corrosive or high-end environments — hotels, food service, healthcare.
- Lenox Plastic Laminate
 Wide color and pattern range for design-forward specifications where privacy and aesthetics both matter.
