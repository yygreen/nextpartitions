# Partitions for Community Centers
 Multi-use community facilities, libraries, churches, and municipal buildings need partitions that balance budget, durability, and easy maintenance.
[Get a Quote](/get-a-quote)
 Community center restrooms get used by everyone — toddlers in summer rec programs, seniors at lunch programs, and weekend event guests. The partitions need to handle moderate-to-high traffic without becoming a recurring maintenance line item, and the spec usually has to fit a tight municipal, church, or non-profit budget.
 We supply community centers, libraries, churches, and municipal buildings with partition packages that balance first cost and service life, including replacement panels and hardware for older restrooms that only need a refresh.
## What Community Facilities Need
- Budget-conscious pricing that survives a board or council review 
- Durability for mixed traffic, from preschool to senior programs 
- Easy cleaning with standard janitorial supplies 
- Child-height options for daycare and preschool restrooms 
- ADA-compliant compartments in every public restroom 
- Colors that coordinate with dated interiors without a full remodel 
- Replacement panels and hardware for partial refurbishments 
- Simple installation that a facilities crew or local contractor can handle

## Planning & Compliance
- Standard stalls are 36 inches wide; ADA compartments are 60 inches wide with a 36-inch outswing door. 
- Youth-program restrooms often use 48-inch panels at 6 to 8 inches off the floor for supervision. 
- Measure wall to wall at both floor level and 30 inches high — older masonry walls are rarely square. 
- Replacing only doors and hardware can refresh a dated restroom at a fraction of a full replacement. 
- Order a spare hardware kit so a volunteer or facilities crew can repair without reordering.
