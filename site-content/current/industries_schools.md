# Bathroom Partitions for Schools
 Vandal-resistant, easy-clean partitions built for K-12 and university restrooms. HDPE solid plastic and phenolic dominate this category for good reason.
[Get a Quote](/get-a-quote)
 School restrooms take more abuse per square foot than almost any other commercial space: kicks, graffiti, hanging on doors, daily disinfecting, and summer deep cleans. Partitions have to outlast all of it without rusting, delaminating, or showing wear that invites more damage.
 We supply K-12 districts, charter schools, and universities with HDPE and phenolic partition packages sized for the room, in colors that hide pencil marks and scuffs. Quotes are stall-by-stall so a district can budget by building.
## What Schools Need
- Vandal resistance — graffiti, kicking, and hardware tampering 
- Panels that can be scrubbed or sanded without changing color 
- Materials that survive daily disinfecting and summer power-washing 
- Height options for elementary versus secondary restrooms 
- Sightline control to deter vaping and bullying 
- Continuous hinges and tamper-resistant fasteners 
- Long warranties that match the district's replacement cycle 
- Colors that hide marks between cleanings

## Planning & Compliance
- Standard stalls run 36 inches wide; ADA compartments are 60 inches wide with a 36-inch outswing door. 
- Elementary: 48-inch panels at 6 to 8 inches off the floor. Secondary: 55-inch panels at 12 inches off the floor. 
- Provide at least one accessible compartment, and an ambulatory stall once the room has six or more compartments. 
- Specify continuous piano hinges instead of gravity hinges to survive door slamming. 
- Order a spare hardware kit up front so maintenance can repair without reordering.
