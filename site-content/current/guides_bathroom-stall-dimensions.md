# Bathroom Stall Dimensions
 Standard and ADA-compliant bathroom stall dimensions for commercial restrooms, including partition heights, door widths, and floor clearances.
[Get a Quote](/get-a-quote)
## Standard Stall Sizes
 | | Stall Type | Width | Depth | Door Width 
 | Standard adult | 36" | 60" | 24" 
 | Narrow / juvenile | 30" | 60" | 22" 
 | ADA wheelchair accessible | 60" min | 56" (wall-hung WC) / 59" (floor-mounted WC) | 32" min clear 
 | Ambulatory accessible | 35–37" | 60" min | 32" min clear 
## Partition Heights & Floor Clearances
 | Standard panel height | 58" 
 | Standard floor gap | 12" 
 | Standard pilaster height | 82" 
 | Floor-to-ceiling pilaster | Custom (matches ceiling) 
 | Privacy panel option | 72–84" with 4–6" floor gap 
## How to Measure Your Restroom
- Measure the back wall length where pilasters and panels will mount.
- Measure the depth from back wall to the front of the stall.
- Identify the ADA stall location — minimum 60" wide, accessible from the entry approach.
- Confirm the door swing direction (outswing required for ADA).
- Verify ceiling height if you're considering overhead-braced or ceiling-hung mounting.
- Photograph the wall and floor conditions and send them with your quote request.
 For detailed accessibility requirements, see our [ADA compliance guide](/guides/ada-compliance). When you're ready, [request a quote](/get-a-quote) with your measurements.
 Disclaimer: ADA dimensions and accessibility guidance shown here are provided for general informational purposes only. Codes and their interpretation vary by jurisdiction and project.
