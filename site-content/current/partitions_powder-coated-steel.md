 The most popular commercial bathroom partition material. Affordable, durable, and available in a wide range of colors. Ideal for offices, community centers, restaurants, and general commercial use.

## Product Overview
 Powder coated steel partitions (also known as baked enamel) are the industry standard for commercial restrooms. A galvannealed steel skin is wrapped around a honeycomb core for rigidity, then baked with an electrostatically applied polyester powder finish that resists scratches, chips, and corrosion. The result is a partition system that hits the right balance of price, durability, and color flexibility for the vast majority of commercial projects.
- Most cost-effective material in our catalog
- 15 standard color choices, custom colors on request
- Honeycomb core for rigidity at low weight
- Powder-coated finish resists scratches and corrosion
- All hardware included — nothing to source separately
- Compatible with all four mounting styles
