## Compare Materials
 Each material offers different benefits in terms of durability, moisture resistance, appearance, and lead time. Powder coated steel remains the workhorse of the commercial restroom industry, but solid plastic HDPE has overtaken it in schools, gyms, and aquatic facilities for its lifetime resistance to rust and graffiti. Phenolic and stainless steel sit at the premium end where vandal-resistance, hygiene, and high-traffic durability are non-negotiable. ### What's included with every order
 Panels, doors, pilasters, headrails, hinges, slide latches, keepers, strikes, brackets, pilaster shoes, and all mounting fasteners. Nothing to source separately.
### ADA-compliant configurations
 Every material is available in ADA stall sizing with 32" clear door openings, outswing doors, and grab bar reinforcement. See our [ADA compliance guide](/guides/ada-compliance).
### Lead times
 Powder coated steel and HDPE in stock colors typically ship in 5–10 business days. Custom colors, phenolic, and stainless run 2–4 weeks. We'll confirm exact dates with your quote.
