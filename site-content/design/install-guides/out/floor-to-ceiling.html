Floor to ceiling partitions: how they go together

Each pilaster is fixed at both the floor and the ceiling, which suits busy restrooms such as schools.

Check the site first

  • Read the manufacturer's instructions and the approved shop drawings from start to finish before drilling anything.
  • Measure the room, wall to wall and floor to ceiling, and compare it with the drawings.
  • Work from the layout drawings, not by eye: they show where each panel meets its pilaster, and it is often off center.
  • Unpack and count every part. Report freight damage straight away, and don't start with parts missing.
  • Floor, walls and ceiling should be finished, clean and free of debris and sticking-out fasteners.
  • Store panels and doors flat, not leaning, and leave the protective film on until the end.
  • Solid plastic (HDPE) needs time to adjust to the room's temperature. Install it in a climate-controlled space, out of direct sun.
  • The ceiling has to have structure above every pilaster position that the top fixing can go into. It is installed by others, before the ceiling is closed.
  • Pilasters are made to the room's height, so measure floor to ceiling at every pilaster position and compare with the drawings before anything is cut or ordered.
  • Solid backing has to be in the walls wherever a bracket is fastened.

Safety

  • Wear proper protective equipment, and check the site for hazards before starting.
  • Panels, doors and pilasters are heavy. Handle them with two people and hold them on solid temporary supports until they are fastened.
  • Don't fasten into a floor, wall or ceiling until you know it can hold the fixing. If the backing or structure isn't there, stop and tell the general contractor.
  • Wear eye protection when fitting spring-loaded hinge pins.
  • Before handover, check that every latch can be opened from outside in an emergency.

Check the room, the ceiling and the drawings

Confirm the room size, the ceiling height at each pilaster position, the structure above the ceiling and the wall backing all match the shop drawings.

2

Lay out floor and ceiling

Mark the pilaster and panel lines on the floor from the drawings, then carry each pilaster position straight up to the ceiling with a plumb line or laser, so top and bottom line up.

After installation

  • Peel off the protective film and clean with mild soap and water only. Acid, tile cleaners and abrasives damage the finish; let the other trades on site know.
  • Leave the manufacturer's care and warranty information with the building owner.
  • Keep the spare hardware and the keys or tools for the latches with the owner.
