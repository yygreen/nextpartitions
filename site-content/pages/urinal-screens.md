# Urinal Dividers and Screens

Urinal dividers, also called urinal screens, are the short panels fixed between urinals for privacy. They come in the same materials as toilet partitions, so they match the stalls beside them, and in four mounting types that suit different walls, floors and ceilings.

## Where they go

![Plan of urinals showing the 15 inch side clearance and 30 inch center spacing](/images/diagrams/urinal-spacing.svg)

The International Plumbing Code sets the spacing around each urinal, and the screens fit inside it:

- From a side wall or partition to the centerline of a urinal: **15" minimum**.
- From one urinal's centerline to the next: **30" minimum**.
- In front of the urinal: **21" minimum** clear.

A screen goes between two urinals, and often between the last urinal and the sink or the entry. The spacing is measured to the urinal centerlines, so confirm the screen size and where it mounts before you set the layout.

## The four mounting types

| Type | How it's held | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Wall hung | Brackets on the back wall only | The simplest type; the floor stays clear |
| Floor anchored | The wall, plus a post anchored to the floor | Larger screens; needs a floor that takes anchors |
| Ceiling hung | The wall, plus a post hung from the ceiling structure | Larger screens with a clear floor; needs structure above the ceiling |
| Post to ceiling | The wall, plus a post running from floor to ceiling | Larger screens braced at the floor and the ceiling |

Every type fixes to the wall, so the wall needs solid backing behind the brackets. Ceiling hung screens also need structure above the finished ceiling, as ceiling hung partitions do.

## Sizes

Sizes vary by manufacturer and material. One manufacturer's phenolic line gives a sense of the range:

| Type | Width | Height |
|---|---|---|
| Wall hung | Up to 24" | 42" to 54" |
| Floor anchored, ceiling hung, post to ceiling | Up to 60" | 58" |

Wall hung screens are commonly 18" or 24" wide and 42" or 48" high. As one example of mounting height, a 42" screen is set with its bottom edge 18" above the floor and a 48" screen with its bottom edge 12" above the floor, each with a 1" gap to the wall.

## Materials

Urinal screens are made in the same five materials as toilet partitions:

![Five partition doors cut through and seen edge on: phenolic, solid plastic, plastic laminate, powder coated steel, stainless steel](/images/diagrams/material-sections-compare.svg)

- **[Solid plastic (HDPE)](/partitions/solid-plastic-hdpe)** and **[phenolic](/partitions/phenolic)** are solid through and handle water, which matters beside a urinal.
- **[Stainless steel](/partitions/stainless-steel)** resists corrosion and suits premium rooms.
- **[Powder coated steel](/partitions/powder-coated-steel)** costs the least; painted steel can rust where the coating is damaged.
- **[Plastic laminate](/partitions/plastic-laminate)** belongs in dry rooms.

Order the screens with the stalls where you can, so the material, color and hardware match.

## What to send for a quote

1. The number of urinals and screens.
2. The distance between urinal centerlines, and from the end urinals to the side walls.
3. The mounting type you want, and what the wall, floor and ceiling are made of.
4. The material and color, or the stalls they should match.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between a urinal divider and a urinal screen?
None. Both names mean the privacy panel between urinals. Manufacturers usually call them urinal screens.

### What size is a urinal divider?
Wall hung screens are commonly 18" or 24" wide and 42" or 48" high. Floor anchored, ceiling hung and post to ceiling screens can be larger, up to 60" wide and 58" high on some lines.

### How far apart should urinals be?
At least 30" from one urinal's centerline to the next, and at least 15" from a centerline to a side wall or partition, under the International Plumbing Code.

### How are urinal dividers mounted?
Wall hung, floor anchored, ceiling hung or post to ceiling. All four fix to the wall, which needs solid backing behind the brackets.

### What are urinal dividers made of?
The same materials as toilet partitions: solid plastic (HDPE), phenolic, stainless steel, powder coated steel and plastic laminate.

### Are urinal dividers required?
The spacing in the plumbing code is measured between urinals with or without screens. Whether screens are required depends on your local code, so ask your code official.
