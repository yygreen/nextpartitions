# About Next Partitions

Next Partitions is a B2B supplier of commercial bathroom partitions, with washroom accessories and lockers alongside. We serve contractors, architects, facility managers and builders across the United States. This page covers what we sell, who we work with, and how a project moves from first message to delivery.

[Get a Quote](/get-a-quote)

## What we do

We supply commercial bathroom partitions and the hardware that goes with them. We carry Bobrick, ASI Global, Scranton Products, Bradley, Metpar, Ammco and Hadrian, and we are an authorized distributor for Bobrick, ASI Global, Scranton Products, Metpar and Ammco.

We sell partitions. We do not install them. You arrange installation with a qualified installer, who should follow the manufacturer's instructions and the approved drawings.

## Who we work with

- **Contractors** who need partitions priced and delivered to a job site.
- **Architects** who need materials, mounting styles and dimensions confirmed before they specify.
- **Facility managers** replacing doors, hinges, latches or whole restrooms.
- **Builders** and owners planning new restrooms.

## How a project works

1. **Tell us what you need.** Use the [configurator](/get-a-quote) to choose material, mounting style, color, number of stalls and dimensions. It is a quote request, not a purchase.
2. **We quote it.** Standard configurations are quoted within 2 business hours; custom projects within 24–48 hours.
3. **Approve and ship.** Custom projects include shop drawings. Once you approve, the partitions ship to your job site by freight.
4. **You arrange installation.** Hire a qualified installer. Give them the manufacturer's instructions and the approved drawings.

## Accessibility

Accessible stall sizing is available in every material. Under the 2010 ADA Standards, a wheelchair-accessible compartment is at least 60 inches wide, with a door that gives at least 32 inches of clear opening (604.8.1.1 and 404.2.3). The configurator adjusts stall sizing when you ask for an accessible stall. Partition options support these dimensions, but compliance depends on your whole restroom, so confirm it with your architect or local code official. See our [ADA compliance guide](/guides/ada-compliance).

*Disclaimer: ADA dimensions and accessibility guidance shown here are provided for general informational purposes only. Codes and their interpretation vary by jurisdiction and project. We accept no responsibility or liability for compliance — always verify requirements with your architect, engineer, or local code official before ordering.*

## Why customers talk to us first

Choosing a partition means choosing three things that depend on each other: the material, how it mounts, and the hardware. A phenolic stall hung from the ceiling needs different support than a powder coated one anchored to the floor. We help you compare options before you commit, then quote the combination you pick. See [materials](/partitions) and [mounting styles](/mounting-styles).
