# How Much Do Bathroom Partitions Cost? (2026 Guide)

How much do bathroom partitions cost? There's no single per-stall price, because a stall isn't one product. It's a set of doors, panels, pilasters, hardware and brackets, made to your room's measurements, in the material and mounting style you pick. Change any of those and the bathroom partition cost changes.

This guide shows what drives the price, how the five common materials rank on cost, what published prices looked like in 2026, and why installation is a separate line. Every price here comes from a public page, with its source and the date we checked it.

## What drives the price

### Material
Material is the biggest single factor. The ranking is in the next section.

### Mounting style
The mounting style decides how many parts you need and what the building must provide. Overhead braced partitions use floor-anchored pilasters and a headrail, and a manufacturer describes the style as economical. Ceiling-hung partitions need structural steel above the ceiling at every pilaster. That steel is designed by the architect or engineer, supplied by another trade and never included with the partitions, so it's a building cost on top of the partition price. Floor-to-ceiling partitions need structural members at the ceiling. Floor-mounted partitions need structural concrete.

### Stall count and layout
More stalls means more parts, but not in a straight line. Stalls in a row share panels and pilasters, so each added stall costs less than the first. The layout also matters. A row between two walls, a row in a corner and a freestanding row need different end parts.

### ADA and ambulatory stalls
Where a restroom has toilet compartments, at least one must be wheelchair accessible, and under the 2010 ADA Standards that compartment is at least 60 inches wide (604.8.1.1). With six or more compartments, or six or more toilets and urinals combined, an ambulatory compartment is required too (213.3.1). A wider stall uses larger panels and a wider door, often with different hardware. See the [ADA compliance guide](/guides/ada-compliance).

We accept no responsibility or liability for compliance — always verify requirements with your architect, engineer, or local code official before ordering.

### Privacy options and height
Privacy upgrades such as no-sightline doors, privacy strips and taller doors mounted closer to the floor change the price compared with a standard 58" door at 12". A manufacturer explains that the standard gapped design spread partly because it is "fast to manufacture, inexpensive to ship, and forgiving to install". [How much privacy options add per stall, from Next Partitions quotes: data from Abe] Some taller doors are only made for overhead braced or floor-to-ceiling mounting, so a privacy upgrade can also change the mounting style. See [why bathroom stalls have gaps](/blog/why-do-bathroom-stalls-have-gaps).

### Hardware
Hardware is part of the price, and it varies by material and line. Published kits we checked came with chrome-plated zamac hardware on powder coated steel, stainless steel hardware on phenolic and aluminum latches and brackets on solid plastic. Ask for any upgrade, such as stainless or continuous hinges, as its own line on the quote. See [hardware](/hardware).

### Color and finish
Standard colors cost less than special ones. A manufacturer notes that special laminates "may increase cost and lead time", and fire-rated solid plastic colors can cost extra.

### Freight
Partitions ship by freight carrier, crated and palletized. They're heavy: a published 3-stall powder coated steel kit weighs 299.7 lb. Freight depends on weight, distance and delivery needs such as a liftgate. [Typical freight range per order from Next Partitions quotes: data from Abe]

### Lead time
Stock colors ship faster than custom work. Published dealer listings we checked gave "usually ships in 10-12 business days" for powder coated steel kits and "usually ships in 20 business days" for a stainless kit. The cheapest material isn't always the cheapest choice. Powder coated steel and plastic laminate suit dry restrooms. In showers, pools or restrooms that get hosed down, they can fail early, and then you pay for partitions twice. Compare all five on the [partitions](/partitions) page.

## Price by mounting style

![The four bathroom partition mounting styles side by side, front view, with the support system in orange](/images/diagrams/mounting-styles-compare.svg)

1. **Overhead braced:** headrail across the pilaster tops.
2. **Floor mounted:** pilasters anchored in the floor only.
3. **Ceiling hung:** pilasters hang from steel above the ceiling, floor clear.
4. **Floor to ceiling:** pilasters anchored at floor and ceiling.

We haven't found a manufacturer source that ranks the four styles by installed cost, so we don't. What changes the total is the building work around them: steel above the ceiling for ceiling-hung, structural concrete for floor-mounted, and ceiling structure for floor-to-ceiling. Check those before you pick a style, because adding steel after the ceiling is closed means opening it up again. Compare the styles on [mounting styles](/mounting-styles).

## Installation: a separate cost, paid to the installer

**Next Partitions sells partitions. We don't install them.** Installation is a separate job done by an independent installer or your general contractor. You hire, schedule and pay them directly, and they price and guarantee their own work. Your partition quote from us won't include installation.

Public records show installed prices, partitions and labor together, but rarely labor alone:

- A June 2025 City of Carpinteria construction estimate for a pool restroom listed "new toilet stall partitions and installation" at $800 per stall and an "ADA-compliant toilet stall partition and installation" at $963. The material isn't stated.
- A 2018 San Antonio Water System bid tabulation for the purchase and installation of HDPE partitions shows two bids of $6,950 and $8,926 for a restroom with 4 stalls and 4 urinal partitions.

[Typical installation price per stall from installer quotes on Next Partitions orders: data from Abe or the job coordinator]

What moves the installer's price:

- **Mounting style and building.** Anchoring into concrete, fixing to steel above a ceiling and working at ceiling height are different jobs.
- **Removal.** Taking out and hauling away old partitions adds to the job when it's included.
- **Wall backing.** Brackets need solid backing in the walls. If it's missing, someone has to add it.
- **Site readiness.** Finished floors, walls and ceilings, clear access and parts delivered complete keep the job to one visit.

Get the installer's price before you commit to a date, and ask them to quote from the approved shop drawings. Read [how to install toilet partitions](/guides/how-to-install-toilet-partitions) to know what the job involves.

## Replacement parts cost

You don't always need a new stall. A single door, panel, hinge or latch can be replaced if the part matches your line. One dealer listed these solid plastic (HDPE) parts on 2026-11-02:

- A 54 1/2" x 55" panel: $713.75

## How to get an accurate quote

![Plan of an empty restroom seen from above, with the stall wall run, room depth, entry door and toilet centerlines lettered](/images/diagrams/measure-new-restroom-plan.svg)

- **A.** Room width along the wall where the stalls go.
- **B.** Room depth, from the stall wall to the opposite wall.
- **D.** Entry door location and which way it swings.
- **E.** Toilet centerlines, measured from the side wall.

Send these with your request:

1. **The measurements above,** plus ceiling height and what the floor and ceiling are made of.
2. **Stall count and types:** standard, wheelchair accessible and ambulatory.
3. **Material, color and mounting style,** or the room's use if you want advice.
4. **Drawings or photos** of the room, or the architect's plans.
5. **Privacy needs,** such as taller doors or no-sightline edges.
6. **The delivery address and date,** and whether you need a liftgate.

## What a partition quote should show

When quotes arrive, check that each one lists the same things, so you compare like with like:

- **Material, line and color** for doors, panels and pilasters.
- **Mounting style** and the layout, ideally with shop drawings.
- **Stall count and sizes,** with the accessible and ambulatory stalls marked.
- **Hardware:** hinge type, latch and the hardware material.
- **Freight:** the delivery address, liftgate if needed and the estimated ship date.
- **Warranty:** the brand's term for that line, and what it excludes.

Installation won't be on a Next Partitions quote. Get it from the installer as a separate price, based on the same drawings.

## Ways to save

- **Match the material to the room,** not to the lowest price. A dry office doesn't need stainless, and a pool shouldn't get laminate.
- **Use standard sizes and stock colors.** They cost less and ship sooner.
- **Choose the mounting style the building already supports,** so no steel or concrete work is added.
- **Put everything on one quote,** so it ships together and you pay for one freight delivery, not several.
- **Replace parts, not stalls,** when the panels are sound.

## FAQ

### How much do bathroom partitions cost per stall?
It depends on material, mounting style, size, hardware and freight. One dealer's 2026 listings ranged from $1,429.80 for a single powder coated steel ADA stall kit to $3,865.75 for three phenolic stalls, before freight and installation. Request a quote for your room.

### What is the cheapest bathroom partition material?
Powder coated steel. In one manufacturer's price index, it's 1.0, with plastic laminate close behind at 1.1. Both suit dry restrooms only.

### What is the most expensive material?
Stainless steel, at about 2.7 times the price of powder coated steel in the same index. Phenolic is next at 2.4.

### How much does it cost to install bathroom partitions?
Installation is priced by the installer, not by Next Partitions. A 2025 city estimate listed partitions and installation together at $800 per standard stall and $963 per ADA stall. Labor depends on the mounting style, the building and whether old stalls come out.

### Does Next Partitions install the partitions?
No. We sell partitions and hardware. You hire and pay an independent installer or your contractor directly.

### Is freight included in the price?
Freight is quoted with the partitions and depends on weight, distance and delivery needs. [Abe: confirm whether freight is always priced in the quote.]

### Can I replace just one door?
Yes, if the new door matches your line, size and thickness. Send photos and measurements. See [replacement parts](/hardware/parts).
