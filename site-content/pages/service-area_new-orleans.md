## The right material for the New Orleans climate

New Orleans gets about 63 inches of rain a year, on roughly 114 days, and relative humidity averages 87% in the morning and 62% in the afternoon. On average the wettest month is June (about 7.6 inches) and the driest is October (about 3.7 inches). What that means for partitions depends on whether the restroom is air-conditioned:

- **Air-conditioned restrooms** (offices, restaurants, clinics, most schools): all five materials work. Choose on looks, cleaning routine and budget.
- **Restrooms open to outside air or without air conditioning** (warehouses, plants, parks, stadiums, pool and locker rooms): choose [solid plastic (HDPE)](/partitions/solid-plastic-hdpe) or [phenolic](/partitions/phenolic). Both are solid all the way through and handle constant moisture.

Keep [plastic laminate](/partitions/plastic-laminate) and [powder coated steel](/partitions/powder-coated-steel) for dry rooms; [stainless steel](/partitions/stainless-steel) resists corrosion. Compare all five on the [partitions page](/partitions).

## The rules a New Orleans restroom has to meet

- **Wheelchair access:** a restroom with stalls needs at least one stall a wheelchair user can enter and use. Federal law sets the sizes, and Louisiana enforces the same federal rules: the State Fire Marshal reviews plans for commercial buildings that are built, renovated or repaired. Ask its Plan Review Division whether your project needs a review.
- **Fire safety:** Louisiana's building rules require solid plastic (HDPE) partitions to pass a room fire test. Ask for the fire-rated version.
- **How many stalls:** Louisiana's rules set the number of toilets by how many people use the building, and state law has every city and parish enforce them unchanged. The table below shows what that means.
- **New rules on January 1, 2027:** Louisiana has scheduled newer editions of its building and plumbing rules to take effect that day. Ask the New Orleans Department of Safety and Permits which edition applies to your project.
- **Louisiana and federal stall rules match:** Louisiana uses the federal access rules for stalls, doors and grab bars, so a stall that meets one meets the other.
- **Single-user and all-gender restrooms:** a one-person restroom must be marked as open to everyone. A shared all-gender room is allowed if every toilet sits in its own compartment with a door and every urinal is in its own room with full-height walls and a solid door. Since 2024, Louisiana law requires public schools, domestic violence shelters, jails and juvenile detention centers to designate each shared restroom for females, males or members of the same family.
- **Permits:** New Orleans requires a renovation permit for construction inside an existing building unless the work is on its exempt list. The list covers interior painting, papering and similar finish work, and replacing cabinets and countertops, and doesn't name toilet partitions. Ask the Building Division at the New Orleans Department of Safety and Permits (504-658-7130) whether your replacement needs one. Plumbing permits come from the Sewerage and Water Board, not the city. You don't need a permit to buy partitions.

Rules change, so confirm your project with the New Orleans Department of Safety and Permits or your architect. The accessible stall's sizes are in our [ADA compliance guide](/guides/ada-compliance), and fixture spacing is in the [restroom layout guide](/guides/restroom-layout).

## How many stalls a New Orleans restroom needs

Louisiana's rules count toilets by a building's capacity: the most people it is designed to hold at one time, worked out from its floor area. Half are counted as men and half as women, and fractions round up. Every toilet needs a stall with a door. Urinals are optional: in each men's room they may replace up to half of the toilets, or up to two thirds in restaurants and schools, and each urinal needs a privacy screen.

:::stalls
head: Office | Restaurant | School
50: 1/1 | 1/1 | 1/1
100: 2/2 | 1/1 | 1/1
200: 3/3 | 2/2 | 2/2
400: 5/5 | 3/3 | 4/4
:::

These are minimums for the whole building, the same statewide because Louisiana cities and parishes enforce the state rules without changing them. At least one stall in each room must be wheelchair accessible. In a school, boys' and girls' restrooms must be on every floor and within 200 feet of each classroom door for children up to age 12, or 400 feet in a secondary school.

To find your building's capacity, the building rules count an office at one person per 150 square feet of floor, a restaurant dining room at one per 15 square feet and a classroom at one per 20.

## What installation labor and sales tax add in New Orleans

- **Local labor rates:** carpenters in the New Orleans–Metairie area earn a median of $24.10 an hour, about 17% below the national median of $29.12. It's a guide to local labor rates, not a quote: installers set their own prices.
- **Public jobs:** Louisiana has no state wage law for public construction, so a required wage applies only on federally funded jobs, at the rate the U.S. Department of Labor sets for the parish. For a carpenter on building work in Orleans Parish that is $19.37 an hour plus $2.46 in benefits.
- **Sales tax:** 10% in New Orleans: 5% state and 5% Orleans Parish. Three small districts add their own tax: 0.245% in the French Quarter district, 1% at Magnolia Marketplace and 2% at Riverwalk-Spanish Plaza.
- **Tax on installation:** in Louisiana, labor to install, remodel or repair part of a building is generally not taxed. The installer pays the tax on the materials it buys for the job.
- **Buying without installation:** partitions shipped to New Orleans are taxable, freight included. If the seller doesn't collect the tax, the buyer owes it.

How tax applies depends on your contract, so confirm with your accountant or the Louisiana Department of Revenue.

### How many stalls does a New Orleans restroom need?
For a building that holds 100 people, an office needs at least 2 women's and 2 men's stalls, a restaurant needs at least 1 women's and 1 men's stall and a school needs at least 1 women's and 1 men's stall.
