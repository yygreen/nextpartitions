# How Tall Are Bathroom Stalls?

A standard bathroom stall door is 58" tall and hangs 12" above the floor, so the top of the door sits 70" from the floor, or 5 feet 10 inches. The panels between stalls are the same height. The posts that hold them up, called pilasters, run higher: 82" to 83" on the most common mounting style, close to 7 feet.

## Standard heights

![Overhead braced toilet partitions, front view](/images/diagrams/mounting-overhead-braced-front.svg)

3. Pilaster: 82" here, some lines 83"

| Part | Typical height | In feet |
|---|---|---|
| Door and panel | 58" | 4' 10" |
| Gap under the door and panel | 12" | 1' |
| Top of the door, from the floor | 70" | 5' 10" |
| Pilaster, overhead braced | 82" to 83" | About 6' 10" |
| Pilaster, floor mounted | 69" | 5' 9" |

These figures are standard across the major brands. Some solid plastic (HDPE) lines differ: 55" doors and panels mounted 14" above the floor, which top out at 69".

## Height by mounting style

The doors and panels stay the same. What changes is how high the pilasters go:

![The four mounting styles side by side: overhead braced, floor mounted, ceiling hung and floor to ceiling](/images/diagrams/mounting-styles-compare.svg)

- **[Overhead braced](/mounting-styles/overhead-braced):** pilasters 82" to 83" high, tied together by a headrail across the top.
- **[Floor mounted](/mounting-styles/floor-mounted):** pilasters about 69" high, level with the tops of the doors.
- **[Ceiling hung](/mounting-styles/ceiling-hung):** pilasters hang from above the ceiling and stop about 12" short of the floor.
- **[Floor to ceiling](/mounting-styles/floor-to-ceiling):** pilasters run the full height of the room.

## Taller stalls for more privacy

Privacy lines use taller doors and panels, mounted closer to the floor:

| Height | Mounted above the floor |
|---|---|
| 64" | 6" or 9" |
| 69" | 9" |
| 72" | 4-1/2" to 9" |
| 73" to 96" | 1" to 4" |

The figures vary by brand and series, and the tallest doors aren't offered in every mounting style. Some lines offer them only overhead braced or floor to ceiling. Full-height stalls run from the floor to the ceiling with no open space above or below. The options are compared in [why do bathroom stalls have gaps](/blog/why-do-bathroom-stalls-have-gaps).

## How low can a stall go?

In a wheelchair accessible stall, the ADA sets a floor under the question. The front partition and at least one side partition need 9" of toe clearance above the floor, so a wheelchair user's feet fit underneath. Stalls designed for children need 12". Larger accessible stalls are exempt, which is why privacy lines often keep the accessible stall's panels at 9" while the others sit lower.

![Front partition, side partition and a section through a partition, with the 9-inch toe clearance under the doors and panels](/images/diagrams/ada-toe-clearance.svg)

The ADA doesn't set a height for the doors and panels themselves. The full rules are in our [ADA compliance guide](/guides/ada-compliance).

## Heights in schools

Secondary schools typically use the standard heights. Elementary restrooms often use lower doors and panels. Heights vary by material and manufacturer, so confirm what is available for the line you choose. More on our [bathroom partitions for schools](/industries/schools) page.

A standard stall is typically 36" wide and at least 60" deep with a floor-mounted toilet, and a wheelchair accessible stall is at least 60" wide. Every size is in our [bathroom stall dimensions guide](/guides/bathroom-stall-dimensions).

## FAQ

### How tall are bathroom stalls in feet?
A standard door is 58" tall and hangs 12" above the floor, so its top is 70" from the floor: 5 feet 10 inches. The pilasters of an overhead braced stall are about 6 feet 10 inches.

### How tall is a bathroom stall door?
58" is standard across the major brands. Some solid plastic lines use 55". Privacy doors run from 64" to 72" and taller.

### Why don't bathroom stalls go to the floor?
The 12" gap lets the floor be cleaned and shows whether a stall is occupied. In an accessible stall, 9" of toe clearance is required under the front and one side partition.

### Can I get bathroom stalls taller than standard?
Yes. Privacy lines offer 64" to 72" doors mounted closer to the floor, and some lines go to 96" or full height. The tallest doors aren't offered in every mounting style.

### How tall are the posts between bathroom stalls?
The posts, called pilasters, are 82" to 83" high on overhead braced stalls and about 69" on floor mounted ones. Floor to ceiling pilasters run the full height of the room.
