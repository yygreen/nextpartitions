# Hadrian Toilet Partitions

Hadrian toilet partitions are commercial restroom partitions made in powder coated steel, stainless steel, phenolic and solid plastic, in headrail braced, floor mounted, ceiling hung and floor to ceiling versions. This page explains what Hadrian makes, how its mounting styles differ, and where to find Hadrian installation instructions. It also covers how we handle Hadrian projects.

## Hadrian mounting styles

- **Headrail braced.** Hadrian describes its anti-grip top rail as giving a rigid and secure installation from top to bottom. It markets its headrail braced stainless partitions for heavy-use applications such as schools, institutions and industrial settings. See [overhead braced](/mounting-styles/overhead-braced).
- **Floor mounted.** Hadrian says the lack of top structure suits high ceiling areas and lends an air of spaciousness. Standard doors and panels in its floor mounted powder coated series are 58 inches high, 12 inches above the floor. See [floor mounted](/mounting-styles/floor-mounted).
- **Ceiling hung.** Hadrian says ceiling hung partitions allow quick and easy floor maintenance, especially with wall-hung fixtures, and suit modernization where a drop ceiling or open span construction can be used. See [ceiling hung](/mounting-styles/ceiling-hung).
- **Floor to ceiling.** Hadrian describes them as extremely strong and resistant to abuse because they connect to the floor, ceiling and walls, and recommends them for high-traffic areas such as schools. See [floor to ceiling](/mounting-styles/floor-to-ceiling).

The [mounting styles guide](/mounting-styles) compares what each style needs from your floor and ceiling.

## Hadrian hardware and parts

Partition hardware does not interchange between brands. Hinge and latch hole patterns, pilaster thickness and fasteners differ, so a Hadrian hinge or latch has to be matched to Hadrian hardware. If you need Hadrian partition hardware or parts, [contact us](/contact) with the series or model number, the material, door thickness, swing and hand, and photos of the hinge and latch. See also [hinges](/hardware/hinges), [locks and latches](/hardware/locks-latches) and [replacement parts](/hardware/parts). We will tell you whether we can supply the part or point you to a matching product.
