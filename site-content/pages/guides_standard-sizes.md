# Toilet Partition Sizes: Standard Panel and Door Sizes

Toilet partition sizes mean the size of the parts: the panels between stalls, the doors, the pilasters and the headrail. They are the numbers you need to order, match or replace a part. Compartment sizes, such as how wide and deep a stall is and what the ADA requires, are on a separate page: [bathroom stall dimensions](/guides/bathroom-stall-dimensions).

Send the part sizes and photos with a [quote request](/get-a-quote). Standard configurations are quoted within 2 business hours; custom projects within 24–48 hours.

## What the parts are

![Parts of a toilet partition, front view](/images/diagrams/partition-anatomy.svg)

1. **Headrail**: the rail across the top of the pilasters on an overhead-braced system.
2. **Pilaster**: the post between stalls that holds the panels and carries the keeper.
3. **Door**.
4. **Hinges**.
5. **Latch and keeper**.
6. **Pilaster shoe**: the cover at the base of the pilaster.

Panels are the dividers between stalls. They are not numbered on the drawing; they run between the pilasters.

## Standard heights

| Part | Standard size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Door and panel height | 58" | Published as standard by Bobrick, ASI Global, Bradley and Metpar |
| Floor clearance | 12" | The bottom of doors and panels sits 12" above the floor |
| Solid plastic door and panel | 55", mounted 14" up | Scranton Products Hiny Hiders; ASI Global No Sight-55 |
| Pilaster, overhead-braced | 82" (Bradley PH400) to 83" (Bobrick) | |
| Pilaster, floor-mounted | 69" (Bobrick) | |

Taller privacy versions exist: Metpar offers 64" and 72" series; ASI Global offers doors up to 72"; Scranton's Eclipse comes in 55", 62" and 72"; Bradley lists 72" at 6" mounting height, or 69" at 9" for ADA stalls; Bobrick's maximum is 71-3/4" at 4-1/2" clearance. See [why do bathroom stalls have gaps](/blog/why-do-bathroom-stalls-have-gaps) for the privacy options.

If you lower the floor gap on the accessible stall, check toe clearance first. The ADA requires 9" of toe clearance above the floor on the front partition and at least one side partition (12" in children's compartments), with exceptions for larger compartments (2010 ADA Standards 604.8.1.4).

## Door widths

Door widths are not round numbers. A door is sized to leave the hinge and latch clearances inside the compartment, so measure the door itself, not the opening between pilasters. Metpar's standard powder coated door widths, for example, run from 21-5/8" to 35-5/8".

An accessible stall door has to leave at least 32" of clear opening (2010 ADA Standards 404.2.3, applied by 604.8.1.2 and 604.8.2.2), so the door itself is wider than 32". A 36" door is a common product size. Standard stalls do not have this requirement and manufacturers size their doors differently. Ask for the door width from your manufacturer's sheet or measure the existing door.

How to measure a door for replacement: [replacement doors](/hardware/doors).

## Panel and pilaster widths

Manufacturers publish panel and pilaster widths on each product's data sheet, and they differ by brand and series. We don't give one number here because there isn't one. Give us the brand, the series and the compartment width and depth, and we'll match them. The standard stall layout, 36" wide and about 60" deep, is covered in [bathroom stall dimensions](/guides/bathroom-stall-dimensions).

## Headrail sizes

On overhead-braced partitions ASI Global lists anti-grip headrails by material: 1-1/4", 1" and 3/4". Match the headrail to the material and brand. See [overhead braced mounting](/mounting-styles/overhead-braced).

## What to send us

1. Brand and series, if known, and the material.
2. Door width, height and thickness, and pilaster thickness.
3. Floor clearance (bottom of the door to the floor).
4. Photos of the whole stall, hinges, latch and keeper.

## FAQ

### What is the standard height of a toilet partition panel?
58", mounted 12" above the floor, on Bobrick, ASI Global, Bradley and Metpar standard lines. Some solid plastic lines use 55" panels mounted 14" up.

### What size are toilet partition doors?
Standard door widths vary by manufacturer. Metpar's standard powder coated doors run from 21-5/8" to 35-5/8" wide and 58" high. Measure the door, not the opening.

### How thick are bathroom partition panels?
It depends on the material: 1" for powder coated steel, plastic laminate and solid plastic panels; 1/2" for phenolic panels with 3/4" doors and pilasters.

### How wide does an ADA stall door need to be?
The opening must be at least 32" clear (2010 ADA Standards 404.2.3). The door leaf itself is wider than that.

### Where are stall width and depth covered?
On [bathroom stall dimensions](/guides/bathroom-stall-dimensions), including the ADA wheelchair, ambulatory and children's compartments.
