# Powder Coated Steel Bathroom Partitions

Powder coated steel bathroom partitions are a steel sandwich: steel face sheets over a honeycomb core, finished with a baked-on powder coating. You will also see them called painted metal or baked enamel partitions. Bobrick's comparison of partition materials credits painted metal with availability, low cost, the option to repaint and a Class A interior finish rating. This page covers how they are built, the published thicknesses and fire class, how warranties differ by brand, and where steel fits.

## How powder coated steel partitions are built

ASI Global describes its powder coated partitions as galvannealed steel with a honeycomb core "for solid construction and sound attenuation", finished with a thermoset hybrid powder coating that resists wear, fading and scuffing. Metpar describes galvannealed steel over a honeycomb core as well.

Published thicknesses (Metpar): 1" doors and panels, 1-1/4" pilasters. Metpar's standard doors are 58" high, with 12" floor clearance. ASI sizes its anti-grip headrail for powder coated at 1-1/4", matching the pilaster.

![Cross-section of a powder coated steel partition door edge: honeycomb core, steel faces, edge](/images/diagrams/material-section-powder-coated-steel.svg)

1. **Core:** honeycomb.
2. **Faces:** galvannealed steel sheets with a baked powder coat finish.
3. **Edge:** where the two steel faces meet around the core.

Hardware is typically chrome-plated zamac (a die-cast zinc alloy). Metpar and ASI both list stainless steel hardware as an option. Metpar's pilaster shoe is 4" high in #4 stainless.

## Fire rating

Bobrick's materials guide gives painted metal a Class A interior finish rating, where Class A is a flame spread index of 25 or less under ASTM E84. That is Bobrick's statement for the material in general. Ratings are published per product line, so ask for the test data for the brand and finish you specify. Powder coated steel is not subject to the NFPA 286 requirement that applies to [HDPE](/partitions/solid-plastic-hdpe).

## Warranty differs by brand

There is no single powder coated steel warranty. ASI Global's catalog lists 3 years for powder coated. The current page said both 1 year and 3 years, and we could not trace the 1-year figure to any manufacturer. Metpar, Bradley, Hadrian and Ammco warranty terms are not in the documents we hold. Your quote will name the brand and its warranty.

## Moisture, vandal resistance and cleaning

- **Moisture.** Steel can rust where the coating is damaged. Bobrick lists rust, dents and scratches as painted metal's weaknesses. Keep it to dry, climate-controlled restrooms; for showers, pools and sustained humidity look at [phenolic](/partitions/phenolic) or [HDPE](/partitions/solid-plastic-hdpe).
- **Graffiti.** Bobrick says graffiti is harder to deal with on painted metal than on other materials.
- **Repainting.** Bobrick lists repainting as a strength.
- **Cleaning.** Follow your manufacturer's care instructions. We don't hold a cleaning specification here.

## ADA stalls

For a wheelchair-accessible compartment the 2010 ADA Standards set a minimum depth of 56 inches for wall-hung toilets and 59 inches for floor-mounted toilets (604.8.1.1). The current page's "60-inch minimum" depth is not the standard's figure. Doors need a 32-inch minimum clear opening and must be self-closing (604.8.1.2). See the [ADA compliance guide](/guides/ada-compliance) and [stall dimensions](/guides/bathroom-stall-dimensions).

## FAQ

### What is powder coated steel partition made of?
Galvannealed steel faces over a honeycomb core, finished with a baked powder coating (ASI, Metpar).

### How thick are powder coated steel partitions?
Metpar lists 1" doors and panels with 1-1/4" pilasters. Measure yours before ordering parts.

### What is the warranty?
It depends on the brand. ASI's catalog lists 3 years. We confirm the figure for your brand at quote time.

### Is powder coated steel fire rated?
Bobrick rates painted metal Class A under ASTM E84. Ask for the test data for your line.

### Will powder coated steel rust?
It can if the coating is breached, according to Bobrick. It suits dry restrooms.

### Can I repaint it?
Bobrick lists repainting as a strength of painted metal. Ask the manufacturer for approved paint and prep.
