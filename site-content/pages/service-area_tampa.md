## The right material for Tampa's climate

Tampa gets about 49 inches of rain a year, on roughly 107 days, and relative humidity averages 86% in the morning and 58% in the afternoon. On average the wettest month is August (about 9.0 inches) and the driest is November (about 1.4 inches). What that means for partitions depends on whether the restroom is air-conditioned:

- **Air-conditioned restrooms** (offices, restaurants, clinics, most schools): all five materials work. Choose on looks, cleaning routine and budget.
- **Restrooms open to outside air or without air conditioning** (warehouses, plants, parks, stadiums, pool and locker rooms): choose [solid plastic (HDPE)](/partitions/solid-plastic-hdpe) or [phenolic](/partitions/phenolic). Both are solid all the way through and handle constant moisture.

Keep [plastic laminate](/partitions/plastic-laminate) and [powder coated steel](/partitions/powder-coated-steel) for dry rooms; [stainless steel](/partitions/stainless-steel) resists corrosion. Compare all five on the [partitions page](/partitions).

## The rules a Tampa restroom has to meet

- **Wheelchair access:** a restroom with stalls needs at least one stall a wheelchair user can enter and use. Federal law and Florida's own access rules set the sizes.
- **Fire safety:** building rules require solid plastic (HDPE) partitions to pass a fire test. Ask for the fire-rated version.
- **How many stalls:** Florida's plumbing rules set the number of toilets and urinals by how many people use the building, and Tampa applies them unchanged. The table below shows what that means.
- **New rules on January 1, 2027:** Florida replaces its building rules with a new edition, and Tampa applies it from that day. A project stays under the current rules if its plans are submitted and its permit application is accepted by December 31, 2026.
- **Florida extras, in new buildings:** the wheelchair-accessible stall needs its own sink inside it, which makes the stall larger than in most states, and the toilet must sit in the corner diagonally across from the stall door.
- **Single-user and all-gender restrooms:** one-person and family restrooms must be marked as open to everyone. A shared all-gender room is allowed if every toilet sits in its own compartment with a door and urinals are in stalls or screened from view. A 2023 Florida law sets separate rules for schools, colleges, jails and government buildings; private businesses aren't covered by it.
- **Permits:** Tampa's list of work that needs no permit covers floor covering, painting, cabinetwork, furnishings and shelving, and doesn't name toilet partitions. The city's permit guide puts bathroom remodels under building alterations, which need a permit and plans. Ask Tampa Construction Services whether your replacement needs one. You don't need a permit to buy partitions.

Rules change, so confirm your project with Tampa Construction Services or your architect. The accessible stall's sizes are in our [ADA compliance guide](/guides/ada-compliance), and fixture spacing is in the [restroom layout guide](/guides/restroom-layout).

## How many stalls a Tampa restroom needs

Florida counts toilets by a building's capacity: the most people it is designed to hold at one time, worked out from its floor area. It isn't the number of staff or of visitors in a day. Half are counted as men and half as women. Every toilet needs a stall. Urinals are optional: they may stand in for up to half of the men's toilets in an office, and up to two-thirds in a restaurant or school.

:::stalls
head: Office | Restaurant | School
50: 1/1 | 1/1 | 1/1
100: 2/2 | 1/1 | 1/1
200: 3/3 | 2/2 | 2/2
400: 5/5 | 3/3;More women's stalls may apply | 4/4
:::

In restaurants and other gathering places, Florida can require three women's toilets for every two men's toilets and urinals once the men's room has more than two. Licensed restaurants may be exempt, so confirm with Tampa Construction Services. Outdoor seating counts toward the number of people. At least one stall in each room must be wheelchair accessible.

To find your building's capacity, the building rules count an office at one person per 150 square feet of floor, a restaurant dining room at one per 15 square feet and a classroom at one per 20. Your architect or Tampa Construction Services sets the final number.

## What installation labor and sales tax add in Tampa

- **Local labor rates:** carpenters in the Tampa–St. Petersburg–Clearwater area earn a median of $25.06 an hour, about 14% below the national median of $29.12. It's a guide to local labor rates, not a quote: installers set their own prices.
- **Public jobs:** Florida has no state wage law for public construction and doesn't let cities or school districts set one. On federally funded work the federal rate applies: $17.37 an hour plus $3.85 in benefits for a carpenter on building work in Hillsborough County.
- **Sales tax:** 7.5% in Tampa: 6% state and 1.5% Hillsborough County. The county's 1.5% applies only to the first $5,000 of each item.
- **Tax on installation:** on a supply-and-install contract for work that becomes part of the building, the contractor pays the tax on the materials and charges you none on the contract.
- **Buying without installation:** partitions shipped to Tampa are taxable. If the seller doesn't collect the tax, the buyer owes it to the state.

How tax applies depends on your contract, so confirm with your accountant or the Florida Department of Revenue.
