## The right material for Virginia Beach's climate

The nearest long-term weather station, at Norfolk, records about 49 inches of rain a year, on roughly 117 days, and relative humidity averages 78% in the morning and 57% in the afternoon. In most Virginia Beach restrooms the room decides the material, not the weather:

- **Air-conditioned restrooms** (offices, restaurants, clinics, most schools): all five materials work. Choose on looks, cleaning routine and budget.
- **Restrooms without air conditioning, or that get wet from the inside** (warehouses, plants, parks, stadiums, pool and locker rooms): choose [solid plastic (HDPE)](/partitions/solid-plastic-hdpe) or [phenolic](/partitions/phenolic). Both are solid all the way through and handle constant moisture.

[Plastic laminate](/partitions/plastic-laminate) has a particleboard core that may swell with excessive moisture, and [powder coated steel](/partitions/powder-coated-steel) can rust where the coating is damaged, so keep both for dry rooms. [Stainless steel](/partitions/stainless-steel) resists corrosion and suits premium public restrooms. Compare all five on the [partitions page](/partitions).

## The rules a Virginia Beach restroom has to meet

- **Wheelchair access:** a restroom with stalls needs at least one stall a wheelchair user can enter and use. Federal law and Virginia's statewide building rules set the sizes. There is no separate state review: your local building department checks access when it reviews the plans.
- **Fire safety:** Virginia's building rules require solid plastic (HDPE) partitions to pass a room-corner fire test. Ask for the fire-rated version.
- **How many stalls:** Virginia's statewide rules set the number of toilets by how many people use the building. A city can't change them, so Virginia Beach enforces them as the state writes them. The table below shows what that means.
- **Virginia extras:** the accessible stall needs more toe space under its partitions than the federal rule asks for: 12 inches high and 8 inches deep, against 9 and 6. It also needs an 18-inch vertical grab bar on the side wall, next to the horizontal one. Every urinal needs a screen on each side that reaches at least 60 inches above the floor, and any stall enclosed from floor to ceiling needs an occupied indicator on the door.
- **Single-user and all-gender restrooms:** a one-person restroom must be marked as open to everyone. A shared all-gender room is allowed in place of separate men's and women's rooms if every toilet sits in its own compartment that runs from floor to ceiling, with gaps of no more than 1/8 inch around the door and an occupied indicator. Urinals there go in the same kind of compartment or in a separate room with a door. Virginia has no state law limiting who may use which restroom.
- **Permits:** the City of Virginia Beach's Permits & Inspections office issues building permits. The city requires one to alter a commercial building, including cutting away any wall or partition. Virginia's statewide rules exempt installing or replacing cabinetry or trim and work the building official considers minor and ordinary. Neither the city's page nor the state's lists name toilet partitions, so ask Permits & Inspections (757-385-4211) whether your replacement needs a permit. You don't need a permit to buy partitions.

Rules change, so confirm your project with the City of Virginia Beach's Permits & Inspections office or your architect. The accessible stall's sizes are in our [ADA compliance guide](/guides/ada-compliance), and fixture spacing is in the [restroom layout guide](/guides/restroom-layout).

## How many stalls a Virginia Beach restroom needs

Virginia counts toilets by a building's capacity: the most people it is designed to hold at one time, worked out from its floor area. It isn't the number of staff or of visitors in a day. Half are counted as men and half as women. Every toilet needs a stall, and Virginia's rules also require a privacy screen on each side of every urinal. Urinals are optional: in a men's room they may replace up to half of the toilets in an office and up to two thirds in a restaurant or school, so the table shows the men's side as stalls.

:::stalls
head: Office | Restaurant | School
50: 1/1 | 1/1 | 1/1
100: 2/2 | 1/1 | 1/1
200: 3/3 | 2/2 | 2/2
400: 5/5 | 3/3 | 4/4
:::

These are minimums for the whole building, and at least one stall in each room must be wheelchair accessible. A men's room that needs only one toilet can't swap it for a urinal. An office designed for 25 people or fewer, or any space for 15 or fewer, may have one shared restroom instead of separate men's and women's rooms.

To find your building's capacity, Virginia's building rules count an office at one person per 150 square feet of floor, a restaurant dining room at one per 15 square feet and a classroom at one per 20.

## What installation labor and sales tax add in Virginia Beach

- **Local labor rates:** carpenters in the Virginia Beach–Chesapeake–Norfolk area earn a median of $24.76 an hour, about 15% below the national median of $29.12. It's a guide to local labor rates, not a quote: installers set their own prices.
- **Public jobs:** on state agency projects over $250,000, Virginia law requires at least the prevailing wage set by the state's Commissioner of Labor and Industry, and a city, county or school division has to pay it only if it has passed its own ordinance. The state bases its rates on the federal ones and may not set them lower. The federal rate for a carpenter on building work in the City of Virginia Beach is $17.93 an hour plus $4.41 in benefits. For a City of Virginia Beach project, ask the city's Purchasing Division whether a wage requirement applies.
- **Sales tax:** 6% in Virginia Beach: 4.3% state, 1% city and a 0.7% regional tax that applies across Hampton Roads.
- **Tax on installation:** in Virginia, installation labor is not taxed when it is listed separately on the invoice. A contractor who supplies and installs materials that become part of the building pays the tax on those materials itself and does not add sales tax to the contract.
- **Buying without installation:** partitions shipped to Virginia Beach are taxable. Freight is not taxed when it is listed separately on the invoice. If the seller doesn't collect the tax, the buyer owes it to the state as use tax.

How tax applies depends on your contract, so confirm with your accountant or the Virginia Department of Taxation.
