## Four ways to mount them

Mounting is separate from material. It decides what your floor and ceiling have to provide.

![The four bathroom partition mounting styles side by side, front view, with the support system in orange](/images/diagrams/mounting-styles-compare.svg)

- **Overhead braced:** floor-anchored pilasters tied together by a headrail. The general-purpose style.
- **Floor mounted:** pilasters bolted into the floor with no headrail. Needs structural concrete.
- **Ceiling hung:** pilasters hang from structural steel above the ceiling, so nothing touches the floor.
- **Floor to ceiling:** pilasters anchored into the floor and the ceiling structure.

## Accessible stalls

Accessible stall sizing is available in every material. Under the 2010 ADA Standards, a wheelchair-accessible compartment is at least 60 inches wide, with a door that gives at least 32 inches of clear opening (604.8.1.1, 404.2.3). Compliance depends on the whole restroom, so confirm it with your architect or code official. See the [ADA compliance guide](/guides/ada-compliance).
