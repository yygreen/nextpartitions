# Bathroom Partition Installation Instructions

Bathroom partition installation instructions come from the manufacturer, and they differ by brand, series, material and mounting style. This page points you to the official document for each brand we carry. Where we could not confirm a manufacturer link, we say so and tell you how to get the document.

Next Partitions sells partitions. We do not install them and are not responsible for installation. Follow the manufacturer's instructions and your approved shop drawings, and use a qualified installer. For a general overview first, read [how to install bathroom partitions](/guides/how-to-install-toilet-partitions).

## Which document to use

1. **Match the series.** Your shop drawings and order paperwork name the brand and series. Pick the document for that series, not a similar one.
2. **Match the mounting style.** Overhead braced, floor mounted, ceiling hung and floor to ceiling have different steps. Many guides are split by style.
3. **Check the date.** Instructions change. Look for a revision date and confirm it with the manufacturer if you have an older copy.
4. **Use it with the drawings.** Read the whole guide before starting, and work from the approved shop drawings.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where do I find installation instructions for my bathroom partitions?
On the manufacturer's site or from the manufacturer's customer service, matched to your series and mounting style. The table above lists the documents we could confirm.

### Are there Hadrian toilet partitions installation instructions?
We could not open Hadrian's own site to confirm a link, so we do not list one. Request the instructions from Hadrian or ask us.

### Where are Bobrick toilet partition installation instructions?
Use the Bobrick installation instructions (form numbers 1080-69, 3080-69, 1550-69, 1040-69, 1540-69 and 1090-69) linked above, matched to your series. If your series is not listed, ask Bobrick or us.

### Do you have Metpar toilet partitions installation instructions?
Yes. Metpar publishes installation instructions for powder coated, stainless steel, Dur-A-Tex and phenolic partitions and an installation manual for Solitude. They are linked in the table above.

### Do you install the partitions?
No. We sell partitions and do not install them. Use a qualified installer.
