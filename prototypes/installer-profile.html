    Tips from before install day

    What the crew asks every customer to sort out first.

    Have the full kit on site

    Shop drawings, the hardware kit and the manufacturer's install sheet. A missing bracket costs a second trip.

    Tell us the floor and wall type

    Tile over concrete needs different anchors than bare slab. Photos of the room help.

    Plan around the building

    For schools and offices we usually work evenings or weekends. Book a week ahead.

    Protect finished floors

    We cover tile around the work area and drill through tape to stop chipping.

    Installing in a DFW school or public building?

    What these jobs usually ask of an installer, in plain language:

    • Proof of insurance naming the school or district as additional insured.
    • Background checks for crews on campus during school hours. Many crews work after hours to avoid this.
    • Accessible stalls laid out to the Texas Accessibility Standards (TAS). Projects of $50,000 or more are registered with TDLR and inspected.

    You don't need a permit or any of this to buy partitions from us. It's what your installer and building may ask for on the job.
