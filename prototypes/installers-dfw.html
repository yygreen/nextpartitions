Hiring a partition installer in Texas

What to expect, what to ask, and the Texas rules that affect commercial restroom projects.

What installation includes

  • Receiving and checking the delivery
  • Layout, anchoring and leveling
  • Doors, hinges, latches and hardware
  • Final adjustment and cleanup

Questions to ask

  • Proof of general liability insurance?
  • Partition installs in the last 12 months?
  • Is removal of old stalls included?
  • How are callbacks handled?

Texas rules

  • Accessible stalls follow the Texas Accessibility Standards (TAS)
  • Many commercial and public projects need TDLR registration and inspection by a Registered Accessibility Specialist
  • Some cities require contractor registration

Typical timeline

  • Quote: 1–2 business days
  • Delivery to DFW: 5–10 business days
  • Install: 1–2 days for most restrooms
  • Final walk-through with your installer
