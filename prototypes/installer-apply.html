    Questions

    What does it cost?

    Nothing. There's no listing fee and no commission on your installation price. Next Partitions earns its margin on the partitions.

    Is it exclusive?

    No. You keep your own customers and other work. The listing agreement only covers customers we refer to you.

    Who invoices the customer for installation?

    You do. You contract and invoice installation directly with the customer. Next Partitions isn't a party to the installation agreement.

    How are jobs assigned?

    The customer chooses among the approved installers who priced their job. The directory lists installers by Installer Score, which combines customer rating, installs for our customers and how fast you reply to job requests.

    When do I get customer contact details?

    When the customer has chosen you and paid for their partitions order. Before that you see the full job packet (scope, drawings, city and ZIP) and message the customer through us.

    Listed installers are independent businesses. Customers hire, schedule and pay them directly. Next Partitions isn't a party to installation agreements and doesn't perform, supervise or guarantee installation work.
