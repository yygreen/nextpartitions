How this affects your listing

Your Installer Score sets your place on the directory and area pages. It combines your rating, installs for our customers and how fast you answer job requests.

  • Slow or missed replies lower your place in the list. If they continue, we review your listing.
  • Ratings below 4.0 or a pattern of complaints trigger a check and can lead to delisting.
  • Reviews are published unedited. You can reply publicly to any review.

Your listing agreement, in short

  • Install the partitions on the customer's Next Partitions order. No substitutes, and no selling partitions on our jobs.
  • Price jobs through this portal and report job status (scheduled, installed).
  • Talk to customers through Next Partitions until the job is released to you. Contact details in messages are hidden until then.
  • Don't go around Next Partitions on customers we refer to you (non-circumvention). Breaches lead to a fee or delisting.
  • You contract and invoice installation directly with the customer. Next Partitions adds no markup to your price.
  • Keep your insurance and business or city registration current. We check them every year.

Summary only; the signed agreement applies. Installers are independent businesses. Next Partitions isn't a party to installation agreements and doesn't perform, supervise or guarantee installation work.
