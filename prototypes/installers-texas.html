      Hiring a partition installer in Texas

      Three Texas rules that affect a restroom project, and how this directory handles them.

      No statewide contractor license

      Texas doesn't license general contractors, so a license number proves little. We keep each approved installer's insurance certificate and business registration on file and contact references. That's a record of what we checked, not a guarantee of anyone's work. Some cities also require contractor registration before they issue a permit.

      Texas Accessibility Standards

      Commercial restrooms in Texas follow the Texas Accessibility Standards (TAS), enforced by TDLR, as well as the ADA. Projects of $50,000 or more must be registered with TDLR and inspected. Ask your installer to confirm accessible stalls, grab bars and door swings follow TAS.

      Sales tax on install labor

      In Texas, labor to remodel or repair a nonresidential building is generally taxable, and labor on new construction generally is not. Ask your installer to show which applies on their quote.
