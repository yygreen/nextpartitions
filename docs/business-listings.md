## 3. Categories

Pick the closest category each platform offers to "supplier of commercial toilet partitions". Primary: a building materials supplier type category. Secondary: bathroom supply or restroom products type categories. Never an installer or contractor category: Next Partitions doesn't install.

## 4. After the listings go live

- **Reviews:** ask every customer after delivery, with a direct review link in the delivery follow-up email. Answer every review, good or bad, within a few days.
- **Monthly check:** search the name and phone, and fix any listing that shows an old or different detail.
- **Track:** calls and website clicks from the Google profile go in the two-weekly report.
