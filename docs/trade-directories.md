# Trade and association directories (researched November 12, 2026)

The trade and association listings from the month 1 plan (Phase 03, "Trade & Association Listings (per 10)", 44 credits). Manufacturer dealer listings are separate: Abe has asked the manufacturers himself.

Every listing uses the same details as the Google Business Profile: **Next Partitions**, (833) 997-7489, https://nextpartitions.com, sales@nextpartitions.com, no street address shown, service area United States. No "authorized dealer" wording until Abe confirms it in writing per brand. No installation categories.

## Check before listing (probably manufacturer-only or paid)

- **Sweets (construction.com):** its 10 21 13 Toilet Compartments section lists "manufacturers and suppliers". Ask whether a reseller can be listed and at what cost.
- **ARCAT:** the 10 21 13 section lists manufacturers, with their specs, CAD and BIM files. Probably not open to resellers.
- **ISSA (the cleaning and facility industry association):** its member directory and the restroom category of the Cleaning Times product directory need a paid membership.

## Later, if Abe wants government and school buyers

- **SAM.gov:** free registration to sell to federal agencies.
- **State procurement portals and purchasing co-ops** (for schools and cities): each has its own registration and bid rules.

## How to submit

Same as Bing: a Claude in Chrome prompt per batch, with the details above. Stop before any payment, password or verification step, and record each listing URL here.
