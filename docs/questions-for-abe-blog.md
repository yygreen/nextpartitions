# Questions for Abe: blog posts (November 3, 2026)

Seven questions, one per blog post. Two to four sentences each, in your own words, from real jobs: a reply, a voice note or a quick call. Each answer goes into a branded "expert advice" box on that post, credited to you. Tell us the name and role to print under it.

1. **What Are Bathroom Partitions Made Of?**
   When a buyer describes their building, what makes you steer them to one material over another, and which detail do buyers tend to overlook?

2. **Why Do Bathroom Stalls Have Gaps?**
   When a buyer asks for no-gap stalls, what's the first question you ask back, and why?

3. **Ceiling Hung vs Floor Mounted vs Floor-to-Ceiling**
   When a buyer has already picked a mounting style, what do you check before you agree it will work?

4. **HDPE vs Phenolic Partitions**
   When a school asks you to choose between HDPE and phenolic, which do you point them to first, and what would change your answer?

5. **Plastic vs Steel Bathroom Partitions**
   In a busy public restroom, what makes you recommend stainless steel over solid plastic, or the other way round?

6. **When to Replace Bathroom Partitions**
   What's the most common reason customers replace their stalls rather than repair them?

7. **How Much Do Bathroom Partitions Cost?** (on hold until your pricing is in)
   When a buyer asks what a stall costs, what do you need from them before you can give a number they can rely on?
