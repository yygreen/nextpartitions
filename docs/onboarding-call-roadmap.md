**Strategy to explain:**
- **Directory model:**
  - Referral only, so no installation sales and no liability for installers' work.
  - How we keep installers from buying around us: partitions quote first, contact details hidden until the customer chooses, listing agreement, trade accounts, call tracking.
- **Labels:** every listed installer is "Approved"; "Established" and "Top rated" are earned extras. They state facts, not endorsements.
- **Installer recruiting (from the call with Joseph):**
  - Screen with "Do you install partitions the customer already bought?"
  - Prefer labor-only crews: Division 10 freelancers, fixture and millwork installers.
  - Find them through job posts and referrals from general contractors and other installers.
- **Content opportunities:**
  - Installer keyword cluster: 147 keywords, about 1,700 searches/month, mostly difficulty 0–5. The biggest wins are the installed-cost guide and the how-to-install guide.
  - Licensing and job-site rules cluster (who may install, school background checks, accessibility review), fed into the city and installer pages.
  - "Toilet partition installers near me" ($6.88 a click in ads, Google shows a map pack) is the directory's main target. Tampa shows demand as a possible next area.
- **Competitor note:** RTI Supply came up as the recommendation in every AI tool for Miami. Worth studying.

**Extras to hand over unprompted:**

- the licensing research,
- the site review issue list (Claude Docs).

## 4. Decisions we need from Abe

1. **Directory model:** is he OK with referral only, no responsibility for installation, and the listing agreement terms (install the partitions on the customer's Next Partitions order, no substitutes, report job status, delisting on breach)?
2. **Approval criteria** and who on his side vets installers, and how much time that takes.
3. **Pilot area:** DFW, or does his order history point elsewhere (e.g. Tampa)?
4. **Trade accounts:** will he give installers trade pricing on partitions, or a rebate?
5. **Install-only requests** (customer bought partitions elsewhere): accept, put behind our own requests, or decline?
6. **Homepage:** the three-ways-into-a-quote version or the facility-picker version, or test both?
7. **Call tracking:** OK to use tracked numbers on installer profiles (small monthly cost)?
8. **Publishing approvals:** who signs off on content, and how fast?
